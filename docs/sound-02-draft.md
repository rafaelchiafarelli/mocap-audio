# SOUND-02 — Draft restraint score brief

## Task and status

- **Task ID:** SOUND-02
- **Status:** Draft in progress / proposal-only
- **Working rule:** this is not approved material. It is a local exploration brief only.

## Purpose

This draft exists to prepare for a creator-approved score exploration, if and when the creator says they want restrained music in the workshop scene. It keeps the exploration narrow, source-grounded, and clearly provisional.

## Confirmed base

The following are already established as working sound principles:

- ticking is selective, not continuous;
- room tone and silence are expressive tools;
- the workshop remains the dominant sonic world;
- any score must not be assumed or treated as approved.

## Why a restrained score is still optional

The brief and worklist are intentionally conservative here:

- the project’s story emphasis is on time, space, and silence;
- a score should remain subtle and support the scene, not dominate it;
- the stronger creative foundation is still the selective ticking and room atmosphere approach.

## Proposed score direction if approved

### Option A — silence-led ambient bed

- no melodic score;
- very sparse tonal texture only underneath key emotional beats;
- use room-space noise and low harmonic ambience rather than a traditional musical bed;
- strongest fit if the film is meant to remain intimate and interior.

### Option B — minimal tonal motif

- a single restrained motif, repeated only at strategic moments;
- extremely sparse instrumentation;
- no dense arrangement, no overt emotional swell;
- strongest fit for transitions between present-day and imagined memory moments.

### Option C — rhythmic pulse without explicit melody

- use a low, textural pulse that feels like time being felt rather than sung;
- not a conventional score;
- only used at rare, carefully chosen moments.

## Local research direction

If approval is granted, the most realistic local path is:

1. test a restrained, quiet ambience prompt with AudioCraft;
2. render short 10–30 second clips only;
3. compare them against our actual scene needs;
4. select one to keep as a prototype reference;
5. document prompt, seed, model version, and render duration.

## Hard thresholds

The following are non-negotiable constraints for any prototype:

- no dense or sentimental score;
- no obvious “cinematic trailer” language;
- no fixed approval claim;
- no output should be treated as final production music.

## Completion condition

This draft is complete when it gives a safe starting point for a creator-approved experiment without pretending a score is already chosen or installed.
